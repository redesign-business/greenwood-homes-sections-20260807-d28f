## About

Greenwood Homes is an award-winning custom home builder founded on the values of quality craftsmanship, unsurpassed customer service, and honest upfront client relationships. Its mission is "quality construction, professionally delivered." Kevin Hanna, President and CEO, built the company on principles from his prior service as a community service provider in the fire department.
